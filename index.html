Satellite-based electrification intelligence for rural ASEAN — detect darkness, prescribe renewables, finance sustained service.

District bundles with satellite-verified milestones — paying for light that persists, not connections installed.
Terang (Indonesian for "light") is a three-layer pipeline that turns open satellite data into investment-grade electrification intelligence. Applied to Maluku province as a proof of concept, the pipeline identifies 111 persistently dark villages and 937 with intermittent supply, prescribes the lowest-cost renewable technology for each, and packages them into bankable district portfolios with satellite-verified disbursement triggers. Prescriptions are cross-validated at 98% against Kementerian ESDM's official resource registry — an independent confirmation that the satellite-derived recommendations align with government-mapped potential.

Demo guidebook

A walkthrough of the five dashboard views and what to look for in each.

1 · Gap map

The entry point. Every village polygon is colored by its VIIRS classification: dark settled, intermittent, stably lit, or data gap. Use the class filter to isolate dark villages. Click any polygon for its Service Quality Index (SQI), radiance, and built-up area.

Toggle ⚡ Off-grid generators to see ESDM-registered installations — 13 sit near villages classified dark or intermittent, suggesting these 2012–2016 systems have degraded below detectable radiance. This is the sustained-service gap the financing model addresses.

Select Labuang in the radiance history dropdown — the January 2017 step-change is an independently confirmed electrification event, detected from radiance alone without ground reporting.

2 · RE prescriptions

The conservation-screened decision tree output. Each polygon is colored by prescribed technology. Filter by tech to focus on one type. The donut chart shows the overall prescription mix.

Toggle 💧 Hydro potential to see ESDM-mapped sites. 98% of micro-hydro prescriptions have an ESDM site within 10 km. Toggle ☀ Solar irradiance and 💨 Wind speed raster overlays to see the resource landscape underneath the prescriptions.

3 · Grid & infrastructure

The central finding: villages are 10.5 km (median) from the nearest lit settlement but 323 km from the nearest formal PLN substation. The 31× gap proves Maluku's electrification is entirely diesel micro-grids, not connected transmission.

Toggle 🔌 Substations and 🏭 Power plants to visualize the infrastructure vacuum. The scatter plot at the bottom maps every village against both distances — nearly all sit far above the parity line.

4 · Bankability ranking

Composite investment-readiness score (0–100) combining additionality, RE resource quality, feasibility, and environmental co-benefit. Use the filters to narrow by kabupaten, technology, or class. The top-50 table shows score components and a SCBA cross-score.

5 · SCBA impact

Four-dimension socioeconomic benefit analysis: reliability (SQI gap), environmental (CO₂ avoidance), household welfare (fuel saving), and employment (construction + O&M jobs). The Kepulauan Aru cold-storage uplift is flagged as a productive-use case study.
